# Cover pictures

Pictures the cover roundel carries in place of a drawn emblem, written by
`python fetch_cover_art.py` and given to books in `books.yaml`. Each is a
public-domain work published by its holder without restriction — the Art
Institute of Chicago and the Metropolitan Museum of Art release these images
under CC0, and the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek under the Public Domain Mark —
so they may be printed on a book that is sold, with no credit owed. They are
credited here all the same.
