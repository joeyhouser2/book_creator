# Catalogue of built books

Every finished book in `output/`, with its author, the date of the original
work, and what it is. Page counts are read from the PDFs themselves, so they
are what KDP would see.

**83 books, 19,236 pages.** Development scraps are listed at the
end and are not books.

The division that matters commercially is **who made the English**. A book with
a published public-domain translation can be sold as it stands. A book whose
English was made by a machine says so on its copyright page, and is worth less
as a result — see [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) for why some works have no other option.

## Known problems

**Over KDP's 828-page interior maximum**, so they cannot be printed as single
volumes. The build warns and continues, but the generated cover has a spine
thicker than KDP accepts, so the rejection arrives after upload:

- **Parzival** — 1253 pp, 425 over
- **Notre-Dame de Paris** — 1240 pp, 412 over
- **Le Rouge et le Noir** — 1124 pp, 296 over

Each needs splitting with `--src-range`, the way Gerusalemme Liberata already
is, which also gives every volume a printable spine.

**Empty output directories**: `pale-fire-la-audio` and
`warsofmarlboroug01tayl-la-audio` — abandoned audio runs.

**`the-gallic-wars.pdf` contains the wrong book.** Page 3 begins Ernest Bramah's
*Kai Lung's Golden Hours*; a Gutenberg id fetched the wrong text and nothing
downstream noticed. The file is a scrap, but the silence is worth remembering.
