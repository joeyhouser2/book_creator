5 · Audiobook — bilingual narration on the GPU

Each bead is read original-then-translation, so the audio follows the printed parallel text. Synthesis is the slow part of a build — measured at about 0.9× realtime, so roughly an hour of GPU per hour of finished audio. Use the test-run field first.
