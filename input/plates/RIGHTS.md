# Period plates for the Black Prince — sources and rights

Each folder holds `original.jpg` (byte-identical to the Wikimedia Commons file;
SHA-1 checked against Commons), `commons.json` (the file's Commons metadata as
fetched 2026-10-12) and `print-gray.jpg` (greyscale, auto-contrast, for the
black-and-white interior).

All four are tagged Public domain on Commons with `Copyrighted = False` and no
attribution required. The tag is Commons' judgement, not a legal opinion: the
holding library's own terms were not read, so see "Still to check".

## Still to check before publication
- BnF Gallica, Besançon and Kortrijk reuse terms for commercial reuse.
- The ring is a photograph of a three-dimensional object; Commons records the
  photographer's public-domain dedication (Jastrow). Confirm on the file page.
- Besançon credit line points to a 2006 culture.gouv.fr page; the shelfmark
  (ms 864 f172) is the stable identifier.

## Derived files
- `crecy-kortrijk-cod135/print-gray-crop.jpg`: the miniature only (top 38% of
  the page, which also carries the text of the treatise), greyscale.
- `ring-louvre-oa9597/print-gray-crop.jpg`: tighter crop of the ring, greyscale.
  At 580 px wide it prints cleanly only below about 1.9 in across.
